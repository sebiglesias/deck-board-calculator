Frequently Asked Questions

What joist spacing is required for composite deck boards?
Standard 5/4 composite decking (Trex, TimberTech) requires maximum 16-inch on-center joist spacing for perpendicular installations. If decking is installed at a 45-degree diagonal pattern, joist spacing must be reduced to 12 inches on-center to prevent board sagging under foot.
Is it cheaper to buy 12ft, 16ft, or 20ft deck boards?
Buying boards matching your exact deck dimensions eliminates butted joints. For a 16-foot wide deck, purchasing 16ft boards avoids unsightly seams in the middle of your deck that can catch moisture and dirt.
Why is joist tape recommended on new deck builds?
Joist tape is a self-adhering butyl membrane placed over the top edge of treated wood joists before laying boards. It prevents rainwater from seeping into screw holes, extending deck frame life by 10 to 15 years.
